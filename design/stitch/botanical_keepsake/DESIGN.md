## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intimacy, permanence, and handcrafted serenity of bespoke wedding stationery brought to life within a modern, mobile-first digital experience. It reconciles two worlds: the tangible, sensory luxury of handmade cotton-rag paper, warm linen, and muted botanical flora, paired with the seamless clarity required for frictionless guest interaction and memory curation.

The visual direction draws from **Refined Tactile Editorial** and **Organic Minimalism**:
- **Tone & Mood:** Warm, dignified, inviting, nostalgic, and effortless. It avoids stark, tech-centric coldness while firmly steering clear of kitsch, artificial neon gradients, and hyper-glossy effects.
- **Physicality:** Interfaces feel as though they are printed on premium heavyweight matte paper (350 g/m² uncoated stock). Cards and modules simulate tactile invitation suites with delicate hairline borders, generous breathing room, and soft ambient light.
- **Audience Context:** Built primarily for smartphone-wielding wedding guests scanning QR codes at reception tables, alongside the couple managing RSVPs, schedules, and shared live photo streams under emotional, time-sensitive conditions.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in European flora, warm alabaster, and untreated stationery. It prioritizes soothing contrast and organic balance:

- **Primary (`#607062` - Muted Sage Green):** Used for focal action points, active indicators, and prominent typographic anchors. Evokes olive branches, eucalyptus garlands, and serene stability. Deepened to `#4D5C4F` for interactive pressed/hover states.
- **Secondary (`#D8CBBC` - Champagne Sand):** A soft, celebratory neutral tone used for structural lines, hairline borders, subtle divider motifs, and gentle chip containers.
- **Tertiary (`#7E9080` - Soft Flora):** Used for supporting accents, secondary tag indicators, and delicate environmental feedback.
- **Neutral Surface Palette:**
  - Base Canvas (`#FAF8F5` - Warm Ivory): The foundational canvas emulating fine unbleached paper.
  - Surface Elevation 1 (`#F5F1EB` - Warm Linen): For nested cards, secondary sections, and input fields.
  - Surface Elevation 2 (`#EFECE6` - Pressed Sand): For subtle card borders, inactive states, and segmented controls.
- **Text & Contrast:**
  - Primary Text (`#232624` - Soft Charcoal): Replaces harsh `#000000` with an organic soot tone, maintaining high contrast without digital glare.
  - Secondary Text (`#3B3F3C` - Deep Lichen): For body descriptions, labels, and timestamps.
  - Muted Text (`#5C625E` - Weathered Stone): For placeholder text, unobtrusive metadata, and disabled iconography.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs the editorial, humanistic cadence of **Playfair Display** with the clear, open geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Editorial Hierarchy:** All prominent titles, romantic couple monograms, program milestones, and celebratory greetings utilize Playfair Display. Weights remain modest (`400`–`600`) to evoke hand-set letterpress rather than heavy commercial advertising. Italic styles are reserved for dates, vows, and tender editorial quotes.
- **Operational UI:** Body text, interactive buttons, form labels, and guest status tags rely strictly on Plus Jakarta Sans. Its rounded apertures harmonize with the soft geometry while guaranteeing legibility under outdoor daylight or low ambient candle-lit reception halls.
- **Letter Spacing:** Headlines preserve standard or slightly tightened tracking, while uppercase pill badges and formal captions leverage gentle positive tracking (`0.03em`–`0.05em`) for timeless stationary elegance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors classical stationery proportions: generous white space (serving as negative visual rest), strict vertical rhythm, and an intuitive handheld experience.

- **Grid Strategy:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column fluid stream with `1.25rem` outer margins. All primary actions reside in the lower 40% thumb-zone.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` outer margins.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column fixed-max layout (`1160px` container) with `2rem` gutters and `4rem` outer margins, centering content to mimic an opened commemorative keepsake album.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Driven by a disciplined `4px`/`8px` scale. Major story breaks and photo collages use generous gaps (`space-xl` / `2.5rem` up to `4rem`) to cultivate calm pacing, preventing informational clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is tactile, optical, and delicate—resembling physical cards layered on top of a linen runner, rather than computational drop shadows.

- **Stationery Paper Stack (Low Elevation):** Cards rely primarily on surface tonal contrast (`#FDFBF7` over `#FAF8F5`) coupled with a crisp 1px hairline border (`#E9DFD3` or `#D8CBBC` at 60% opacity).
- **Ambient Paper Glow (Medium Elevation):** Used for interactive modal sheets, floating guest upload buttons, and active photo tiles. Achieved using a warm, diffused shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(77, 92, 79, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(35, 38, 36, 0.03);`
- **Floating Controls (High Elevation):** Mobile sticky bars and RSVP drawers float slightly above the canvas with an added backdrop blur:
  - `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -6px rgba(77, 92, 79, 0.12);`
  - `backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.88);`
- **Zero Heavy Black Shadows:** Shadows are tinted with `#4D5C4F` (deep sage) to sustain warm organic resonance without visual grime.

## Shapes

The shape system expresses gentle warmth through rounded corners reminiscent of die-cut paper, curved archival framing, and smooth river stones.

- **Base Corner Radii:**
  - Standard cards and surfaces: `1rem` (rounded-lg) to `1.5rem` (rounded-xl) for a welcoming, organic impression.
  - Modals, prominent memory highlight cards, and sticky bottom sheets: `1.5rem` to `2rem` (rounded-2xl).
- **Pill Morphology:** Interactive buttons, state chips, audio player bars, and tag badges feature full pill radii (`9999px`) to invite touch and reinforce the friendly hand-held feel.
- **Framing Details:** Photography frames adopt smooth rounded corners (`1.25rem`), sometimes flanked by subtle inset borders (`border: 4px solid #FAF8F5`) mimicking fine museum-mount mat boards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., „Glückwunsch senden“, „Foto hochladen“):** Full pill shape, filled with `#607062` (Sage), typography in `#FAF8F5` (`label-lg`), minimum touch height of `48px` (mobile-optimized). Hover/press state smoothly transitions to `#4D5C4F`.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., „Details ansehen“):** Pill container with `#FAF8F5` or transparent background, bounded by a 1px solid `#D8CBBC` border, text in `#232624`.
- **Text Link Button:** Understated text in `#607062` featuring an offset underline with a 1px flourish accent.

### Wedding Stationery Cards
- **Architecture:** Crisp `#FDFBF7` surface layered against the `#FAF8F5` canvas. Finished with a 1px perimeter border in `#E9DFD3` and an inner padding of `1.5rem`.
- **Variants:**
  - *Program Card:* Features a Playfair Display timestamp aligned to an organic dotted timeline guide.
  - *Memory / Photo Card:* Framed with balanced white mat-margins, soft rounded corners, and contextual caption space underneath.

### Status Pills & Badges
- **Form:** Compact pill shape (`height: 28px`), `label-caption` uppercase tracking, padding `0 12px`.
- **States:**
  - *Offen (Pending):* Background `#FAF8F5`, border 1px solid `#D8CBBC`, text `#5C625E`.
  - *Erledigt (Accepted / RSVP Yes):* Background `#EFECE6`, border 1px solid `#607062`, text `#4D5C4F`.
  - *Freigeschaltet (Approved / Live):* Background `#607062`, text `#FAF8F5`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Inputs:** Soft `#F5F1EB` background, 1px border in `#D8CBBC`, corner radius `0.75rem`, text `#232624`. Focus transition: 1.5px border in `#607062` accompanied by a muted sage aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(96, 112, 98, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circle-based for radios and soft-square (`rounded-sm`) for checkboxes. Inactive state: `#D8CBBC` border. Selected state: solid `#607062` with crisp off-white glyphs.

### Mobile Navigation & Floating Actions (QR Entry Point)
- **Floating Action Button (FAB):** A sticky, centrally positioned mobile camera/upload button in `#607062` with a subtle paper-shadow, ensuring guest uploads require exactly one tap upon QR scan.
- **Bottom Navigation Sheet:** Translucent warm ivory with soft blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), rounded-t-3xl corners, and safe-area inset accommodation for iOS and Android.
